- Annualised CAGR ≈ full_sum / 6 / N_concurrent_positions, very roughly.
- `full_3x_compd` is naive sequential compounding — use as relative ranking, not absolute.
- We're looking for: PF ≥ 1.5, both H2 and Q1 PF ≥ 1.2, and consistent across tiers.
